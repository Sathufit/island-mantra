Getting Started — Section 1

Logging In & Dashboard

The admin panel is the control centre for your entire store. Everything — from adding a new product to confirming a shipped order — is managed here.

island-mantra-backend.onrender.com/app
First-time login

Enter your admin email and password. If you have forgotten your password, contact your developer to reset it from the server. Standard password-reset emails require email configuration which may not be enabled yet.

Dashboard Overview

After logging in you land on the Dashboard. This gives a quick snapshot of store performance:

Orders Summary

Total orders placed, pending fulfillment, and recently completed — updated in real time.

Revenue

Gross revenue for the current period displayed in LKR (Sri Lankan Rupee), your store's base currency.

Products

Total published products and any that are in draft (not yet visible on the storefront).

Customers

Registered customers and guests who have placed orders.

Daily Operations — Section 3

Orders

Orders are the core of daily operations. This section covers how to view, process, fulfil, and handle exceptions for every order that comes in.

Orders

Order List

The orders list shows every order, newest first. Each row shows the order number, customer name, date, total, payment status, and fulfillment status.

Order Statuses

Pending Processing Completed Cancelled Archived

Payment Statuses

Awaiting Captured Refunded Not Paid

Processing an Order — Step by Step

1
Open the order Click any row in the Orders list to open the order detail page.
2
Confirm payment Check the Payment section. For cash-on-delivery or manual bank-transfer orders, click Capture Payment once you have confirmed receipt of funds.
3
Fulfil the order In the Fulfillment section, click Create Fulfillment. Select the items to ship (all or partial) and confirm. This marks items as shipped.
4
Add a tracking number (optional) After fulfillment is created, you can add a courier tracking number for the customer's reference.
5
Mark as shipped Click Mark Shipped on the fulfillment. The order status moves to Completed.

Order Detail — What Each Section Means

SectionDescription
SummaryLine items, quantities, unit prices, subtotal, shipping, and total in LKR
PaymentPayment provider used, amount captured, and any refunds issued
FulfillmentShipping method, fulfillment status, and tracking information
CustomerName, email, shipping address, and link to their full profile
TimelineAudit log of every action taken on this order with timestamps

Cancelling an Order

Open the order, click the ⋯ More menu in the top-right corner, and select Cancel Order. A cancellation can only be done before the order is fulfilled. If payment was captured, issue a refund first.

Issuing a Refund

1
Open the order → Payment section Click Refund.
2
Enter the refund amount You can refund the full amount or a partial amount (e.g. for a single returned item).
3
Add a reason note Always add a note (e.g. "Customer returned defective item") for your records.
Important

Refunds in the admin are recorded entries only. The actual money transfer back to the customer must be done manually through your bank or payment provider unless you have an automated payment gateway connected.

Daily Operations — Section 4

Customers

The Customers section holds every registered account and guest buyer. Use it to look up order history, verify addresses, or assist customers with account issues.

Customers

Customer List

Each row shows the customer's name, email, number of orders, and the date they registered. Use the search bar to find a customer by name or email instantly.

Customer Profile

Clicking a customer opens their full profile, which contains:

SectionWhat You'll Find
OverviewName, email, phone number, and account creation date
OrdersFull list of every order this customer has placed, with statuses and totals
AddressesSaved delivery addresses on the account
Guest Customers

Orders placed without creating an account appear as guest entries. They have order history but no saved addresses or login credentials.

Editing a Customer

From the customer profile, click Edit to update their name, email, or phone. You cannot change their password from here — password resets are customer-initiated from the storefront.

Catalogue Management — Section 5

Products

Products are the heart of your store. This section covers everything from adding a new item to managing variants, images, and metadata that power the storefront.

Products

Adding a New Product

1
Click "Create Product" The button is in the top-right corner of the Products list.
2
Fill in General Information Title, subtitle, description, handle (URL slug), and material. The handle auto-generates from the title but can be customised.
3
Organise the product Assign a Collection (e.g. "Hikkaduwa Summer") and a Category (e.g. "Women"). Both control how the product appears in the storefront navigation.
4
Add Options (variants) Define the options that create variants. For Island Mantra, these are typically Colour and Size.
5
Set prices per variant Each variant needs a price in LKR. Click into a variant row and set the price under the Sri Lanka region.
6
Upload images Drag and drop product images. The first image becomes the thumbnail. Recommended: upload 3–6 images per product at 1:1 or 4:5 ratio.
7
Publish Toggle the product status from Draft to Published. Only published products appear on the storefront.

Product Fields Reference

FieldDescription
Title RequiredThe product name shown to customers, e.g. "Coral Reef Sundress"
Handle RequiredURL-friendly identifier, auto-generated. e.g. coral-reef-sundress. Cannot contain spaces.
Description OptionalFull product description shown on the product page
Material OptionalFabric content shown in the Fabric & Care section on the storefront
Collection OptionalLinks this product to a named collection (appears in navigation and carousels)
Categories OptionalAssigns the product to a category like Women or Accessories
StatusDraft (hidden) or Published (live on storefront)

Managing Variants

Each combination of options (e.g. Blue / Small, Blue / Medium, Red / Small) becomes a variant. Each variant has its own:

  • SKU — Stock keeping unit, your internal reference code
  • Price — Set per region (Sri Lanka / LKR)
  • Inventory — Tracked stock level for this specific size and colour
  • Metadata — Custom fields used by the storefront (e.g. colour hex code for the colour swatch)
Colour Swatches

The storefront displays colour swatches using a colorHex metadata key on each variant. When adding a new colour variant, add a metadata entry with key colorHex and value #RRGGBB (e.g. #C8856A for terracotta).

Editing an Existing Product

Click any product in the list to open it. All fields are editable in-place. Changes save automatically when you click Save at the top of the form. Changing the handle after the product is live will break any saved or shared links — avoid unless necessary.

Deleting a Product

Open the product → click the ⋯ More menu → Delete. This is permanent. If a product is just temporarily unavailable, set it to Draft instead of deleting it.

Catalogue Management — Sections 6 & 7

Collections & Categories

Collections and categories both organise products, but serve different purposes. Understanding the difference is important before making changes.

Collections

Collections

A collection is a curated editorial group — think of it as a seasonal campaign or a story. Products can belong to one collection. Collections appear in the storefront navigation and power the product carousels on the homepage.

Examples: "Hikkaduwa Summer", "Rainy Season Edit", "Surf & Shore"

Creating a Collection

1
Click "Create Collection" Enter a title and an optional handle (auto-generated if left blank).
2
Add products Once created, use the Add Products button to assign existing products to this collection.

Categories

Categories

A category is structural — it mirrors the top-level navigation of your storefront. Categories represent the type or gender of clothing, not a campaign. Products can belong to one category.

Current categories: Women, Men, Kids, Accessories

Do Not Rename Categories

The storefront navigation is tied to the category handles (women, men, kids, accessories). Renaming or deleting a category will break its storefront page. Only add new categories — do not modify existing ones without informing your developer.

Catalogue Management — Section 8

Inventory

Inventory tracks how many units of each variant are in stock. Keeping this accurate ensures customers cannot purchase items you cannot fulfil.

Inventory

How Inventory Works

Each product variant (e.g. Blue Sundress / Size M) has its own inventory item. When a customer places an order, the reserved quantity increases. When you fulfil the order, the stock is deducted.

TermMeaning
In StockTotal units physically available at your location
ReservedUnits held for orders that are placed but not yet fulfilled
AvailableIn Stock minus Reserved — what customers can still order

Updating Stock Levels

1
Go to Inventory Use the search bar to find the variant by name or SKU.
2
Click the inventory item Opens the detail view showing stock per location.
3
Edit the quantity Click the quantity field next to your location and enter the new stock count. Click Save.
Best Practice

Update inventory immediately after receiving new stock from your supplier, and again after any in-store sales or manual adjustments. Accurate stock prevents overselling and disappointing customers.

Out-of-Stock Behaviour

When a variant's available quantity reaches 0, that size/colour combination becomes unselectable on the storefront. The product page still shows but that specific variant is greyed out automatically — no action needed from you.

Pricing & Promotions — Section 9

Price Lists

Price lists let you create special pricing rules that override the standard product price — perfect for sale events, seasonal discounts, or wholesale pricing.

Price Lists

Types of Price Lists

Sale

A temporary price reduction on selected products or variants. Runs between two dates and automatically reverts after the end date.

Override

Permanently replaces the standard price for specific customer groups (e.g. wholesale or VIP buyers).

Creating a Sale Price List

1
Click "Create Price List" Choose type Sale, give it a name (e.g. "August End-of-Season Sale"), and set a start and end date.
2
Add products Click Add Products and select the items you want to discount.
3
Set the sale price per variant For each variant, enter the discounted price in LKR. This is the exact price, not a percentage — so if the item is Rs 5,000 and you want 20% off, enter Rs 4,000.
4
Save and activate The price list activates automatically on the start date. The storefront shows the sale price and a strikethrough of the original price.
Compare-At Price

The strikethrough "was" price visible on the storefront comes from the variant's standard price when a sale price list is active. No extra setup needed — it shows automatically.

Pricing & Promotions — Section 10

Promotions & Discount Codes

Promotions let you create discount codes customers enter at checkout, or automatic discounts that apply without a code.

Promotions

Creating a Discount Code

1
Click "Create Promotion" Select Discount Code as the type.
2
Enter the code Type the code customers will use at checkout (e.g. WELCOME10). Codes are case-insensitive.
3
Set the discount value Choose Percentage (e.g. 10%) or Fixed Amount (e.g. Rs 500 off). For free shipping, select Free Shipping.
4
Set conditions (optional) Minimum order value, applicable products or categories, and usage limits (e.g. one use per customer, or 100 uses total).
5
Set validity dates (optional) Codes without an end date run indefinitely until manually disabled.

Discount Types Reference

TypeHow It WorksExample
PercentageReduces the cart total by a %10% off entire order
Fixed AmountDeducts a set rupee amount from the totalRs 1,000 off orders over Rs 5,000
Free ShippingWaives the shipping charge at checkoutFREESHIP code for newsletter subscribers

Disabling a Code

Open the promotion → toggle the Active switch off. The code immediately stops working at checkout. You can re-enable it at any time.

Configuration — Sections 11–14

Settings

Settings control the foundational configuration of your store. Most of these are set up once and rarely changed — but it's important to know where everything lives.

Settings

Store Settings

Found under Settings → Store. Contains:

  • Store Name — "Island Mantra" — shown in emails and receipts
  • Default Currency — LKR (Sri Lankan Rupee)
  • Default Region — Sri Lanka

Regions & Currency

Found under Settings → Regions. Island Mantra operates a single region (Sri Lanka in LKR). The region controls:

  • Which currency prices are displayed and charged in
  • Which payment providers are available
  • Which shipping options appear at checkout
  • Tax calculation rules
Expanding to new markets

If you ever want to sell in another currency (e.g. USD for international customers), a new region would be created and linked to new shipping options. This requires developer involvement.

Shipping Options

Found under Settings → Regions → Sri Lanka → Shipping Options. Each shipping option has:

FieldDescription
NameShown to the customer at checkout (e.g. "Standard Delivery")
PriceFlat rate in LKR charged for this shipping method
ProviderThe fulfilment provider handling the delivery (manual by default)

To add or change a shipping rate, click into the region → Add Shipping Option or click an existing option to edit its price. Changes take effect immediately at checkout.

Team & User Management

Found under Settings → Team. From here you can:

  • Invite a new admin user — Enter their email address. They'll receive an invitation link to set their own password.
  • View existing team members — All users who have access to the admin panel are listed here.
  • Remove a user — Click the user row and select Remove to revoke their access immediately.
Access Control

All admin users currently have full access. Only invite people who genuinely need to manage the store. Remove users promptly when they no longer need access.

API Keys (Publishable Keys)

Found under Settings → API Key Management. The Publishable API Key is what connects your storefront (island-mantra.netlify.app) to the backend.

Do Not Delete Active Keys

Deleting or rotating the publishable key will immediately break the storefront — products will not load, and customers cannot add to cart or check out. If you need to rotate the key, coordinate with your developer first so the new key is updated in Netlify's environment variables at the same time.

Quick Reference

Daily Checklist & Common Tasks

A one-page reference for the most frequently performed tasks in the admin.

Every Day

  • Check Orders for any new or pending orders and process them
  • Confirm payment has been captured on completed bank-transfer orders
  • Mark fulfilled orders as Shipped once dispatched

When Stock Arrives

  • Go to Inventory → find each variant → update the quantity
  • If it's a brand new product, create it in Products first, then update inventory

When Running a Sale

  • Create a Price List (type: Sale) with start and end dates
  • Add the products and enter the sale price per variant
  • Verify on the storefront that the strikethrough price shows correctly
